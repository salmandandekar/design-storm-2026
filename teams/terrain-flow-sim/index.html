Where the water goes, and how fast

A physics-labelled simulation of Denver Water's South Platte collection system on real terrain: snowmelt and rain seed particles that follow the land downhill, join the river, cross the reservoir and reach the Foothills plant. Every number says whether it was computed from the files here, assumed, or is general knowledge. Back to the system map.
