How to move around
Pan
Drag with the left mouse button, or one finger on a touchscreen.
Zoom
Scroll, pinch, or the + and − buttons at top right.
Rotate and tilt
Drag with the right mouse button, or hold Ctrl and drag. On a trackpad, drag with two fingers. The compass at top right does it too, and clicking the compass snaps back to north.
